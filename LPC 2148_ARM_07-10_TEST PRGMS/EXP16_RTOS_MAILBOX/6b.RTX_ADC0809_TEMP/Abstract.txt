ADC0809 is implemented using Mailbox concept in this RTX example init_task will create 
send_task<2>, rec_task<1> and delete itself from kernel.

Mailbox is created with name T_MEAS both send_task and rec_task will share this mailbox 
to transfer data.

ADC converted temperature values are manipulated in send_task and displayed in rec_task. LCD is used 
for display purpose.
