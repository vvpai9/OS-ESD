DeadLock:

Task1 which is waiting on semaphore Arrived1 arrives first, it will block at its wait.

Task2 which is waiting on semaphore Arrived2 arrives, it will also block, 

since task1 wasn't able to signal Arrived1. At this point, neither thread can proceed
and never will This situation is called a deadlock and obviously, it is not a successful
solution of the synchronization problem. In this case the error is obvious.
