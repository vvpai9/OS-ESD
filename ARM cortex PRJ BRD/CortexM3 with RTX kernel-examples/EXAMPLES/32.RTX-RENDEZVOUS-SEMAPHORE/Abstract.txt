Rendezvous:
A more generalized form of semaphore signaling is a rendezvous. 
A rendezvous ensures that two tasks reach a certain point of execution. 
Neither may continue until both have reached the rendezvous point.

In the example above, the two semaphores ensure that both tasks will rendezvous
and proceed then to execute FuncA2() and FuncB2().
